### Curated by `@codewithboi`

If I had to relearn data engineering with zero euros, these are the only 5 I'd use. Every resource below is 100% free.

## 1. Data Engineer Handbook
**DataExpert-io / Zach Wilson** · GitHub · All levels

**What you will learn:** Where to find the best books, courses, communities, newsletters, project ideas and interview prep for every stage of a data engineering career. It also points you to the engineering blogs of companies that run data at massive scale.

**Value:** Someone has already filtered the noise, so you get a curated map of the field instead of endless Google searches. Bookmark it and pick one thing at a time, don't binge it.

**Link:** https://github.com/DataExpert-io/data-engineer-handbook

---

## 2. Distributed Systems Lecture Series
**Martin Kleppmann, University of Cambridge** · YouTube · Advanced

**What you will learn:** How systems behave when data lives on many machines, covering replication, consistency, logical time, consensus, and real case studies like Google's Spanner. It's about 7 hours of video with a free set of lecture notes.

**Value:** It's taught by the author of *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*, so you get the thinking behind the "data engineering bible" for free. Once it clicks, you understand why tools like Kafka, Spark and cloud warehouses behave the way they do.

**Link:** https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLeKd45zvjcDFUEv_ohr_HdUFe97RItdiB

---

## 3. Data Engineering Zoomcamp
**DataTalksClub** · Free course · Beginner to Intermediate

**What you will learn:** How to build a full data platform over 9 weeks with Docker, Terraform, workflow orchestration, BigQuery, dbt, Spark and Kafka. You finish with a peer-reviewed final project.

**Value:** You leave with a portfolio project that uses the exact tools in job descriptions. Join the free live cohort (usually starting in January) for a certificate, or go self-paced anytime.

**Link:** https://github.com/DataTalksClub/data-engineering-zoomcamp

---

## 4. Data Engineering Weekly
**Ananth Packkildurai** · Newsletter · Intermediate to Advanced

**What you will learn:** How real companies build and run their data platforms, from lakehouse formats like Apache Iceberg to data contracts, data quality and streaming. Ananth also writes essays that question industry hype.

**Value:** A few minutes a week keeps you current without doomscrolling. It's how senior engineers spot which ideas are gaining traction across the industry.

**Link:** https://www.dataengineeringweekly.com

---

## 5. Seattle Data Guy
**Ben Rogojan** · YouTube + newsletter · All levels

**What you will learn:** What the data engineering job is really like, including breaking into the field, interview prep, data strategy, choosing tools, and how to start freelancing. Ben is an ex-Facebook data engineer who now runs his own consulting company.

**Value:** You get the career and business side that tutorials never cover. It's especially useful for career changers and anyone thinking about going independent.

**Link:** https://www.youtube.com/@SeattleDataGuy · Newsletter: https://seattledataguy.substack.com

Start the **Zoomcamp** to build, keep the **Handbook** bookmarked as your map, and move to **Kleppmann's lectures** once you've shipped a project. Run **Data Engineering Weekly** and **Seattle Data Guy** alongside the whole way.
